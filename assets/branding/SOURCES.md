# Branding asset sources

Not part of the app's runtime behavior - kept here so the provenance of each
image is auditable.

- `curent_logo.png`, `curent_icon.png` - CURENT (Center for Ultra-Wide-Area
  Resilient Electric Energy Transmission Networks), an NSF/DOE Engineering
  Research Center headquartered at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville.
  Downloaded from the official site's public asset CDN
  (`curent.utk.edu/wp-content/uploads/...`). Used with the CURENT-affiliated
  maintainer's authorization as this app's own origin attribution.
- `tva_logo.png` - Tennessee Valley Authority. Rasterized from the SVG at
  Wikimedia Commons (`US-TennesseeValleyAuthority-Logo.svg`), a work of the
  U.S. federal government and public domain from a copyright standpoint.
  TVA's name and mark carry trademark protection (Reg. No. 74512014); this
  app uses it only as a factual development-partner attribution, not as an
  endorsement claim or commercial product mark.
